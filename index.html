Got Questions?

We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Do you take walk-ins? +
Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome. For the shortest wait, give us a call or text ahead so we can let you know the best time to swing by.
How long does a kids cut take? +
Most kids cuts take about 20–30 minutes. We go at a comfortable pace so little ones don't feel rushed — and we've got plenty of tricks to keep them happy!
What ages do you serve? +
We cut hair for all ages — from toddlers getting their very first trim to adults looking for a fresh style. Everyone is welcome at Little Clippers!
Do you cut curly or textured hair? +
Absolutely! Our stylists have experience with all hair types including curly, wavy, thick, and textured hair. Just let us know what you're going for.
How much does a haircut cost? +
Pricing varies by service. Give us a call at (830) 778-5191 for current pricing — we keep it fair and affordable for the whole family.
